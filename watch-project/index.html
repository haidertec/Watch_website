A Little About Us

Alberto started in 1881 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, a small Swiss city where almost everyone worked in watchmaking. Rudolf Albert Riehle started by buying, repairing, and reselling pocket watches. By 1883 he had registered the business and started making his own watches in a small workshop. In 1903, his son Julien moved everything to Geneva to be closer to the luxury market. That move changed everything — from simple pocket watches to refined timepieces. Today the brand still makes watches in Switzerland, with the same focus on clean design and reliable movements. We're not the biggest name out there, but we've been doing this for over 140 years.
