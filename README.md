# ⌚ Alberto Watch Co. — Luxury Watches Showcase

A sleek, responsive one-page luxury watch e-commerce landing page crafted for **Alberto Watch Co.** (Handmade in Switzerland since 1881). Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, featuring a dark gold-accented aesthetic, category filtering, and interactive product quick-view modals.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Luxury Dark & Gold UI:** Designed with a deep obsidian background (`#0a0a0c`), warm gold accents (`#c5a059`), and classic serif typography (`Georgia`).
* **Fixed Navigation & Category Dropdown:** Smooth-scrolling navigation bar with a category filter menu (*Smart*, *Sport*, *Latest*, *Limited Edition*).
* **24-Watch Collection Grid:** Responsive 3-column product showcase displaying high-end timepieces with hover zoom effects, descriptions, and pricing.
* **Interactive Quick-View Modal:** Clicking any watch card or **View** button opens a detailed popup modal with full specifications and pricing.
* **Brand Heritage Section:** Tells the story of Alberto Watch Co. from its founding in 1881 in La Chaux-de-Fonds to Geneva.
* **Contact & Store Info:** Includes an interactive inquiry form alongside business contact details and working hours.
* **Fully Responsive:** Adapts seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens (`992px`, `768px`, `550px`, and `360px` breakpoints).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **HTML5** — Semantic structure and modal layout
* **CSS3** — Custom properties, CSS Grid, Flexbox, keyframe animations (`fadeUp`), and responsive media queries
* **Vanilla JavaScript** — Category filtering, dropdown toggles, and modal popup interactions
